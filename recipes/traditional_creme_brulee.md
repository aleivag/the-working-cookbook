---
title: Traditional Crème Brûlée
yield: 6 servings
headnote: Silky vanilla custards baked gently in a water bath and finished with a brittle caramel crust.
family: creme-brulee
tags: [dessert, custard, baked, make-ahead, french, egg-yolks]
see_also: [sous-vide-creme-brulee]
---

== step prepare ==
Adjust an oven rack to the middle position and preheat the #oven{} to
$temp{325%F}. Place 6 shallow #ramekins{} in a #rimmed roasting pan{}.

Separate @large eggs{5}[scale=true, step=1] into =>egg yolks{5} and
=>egg whites{5}. Save the egg whites for another recipe like
[pavlova](recipe:orange-cranberry-mint-pavlova).

== step heat cream ==
Combine @heavy cream{2%cups}, @vanilla extract{1%tsp}, and @salt{}(a pinch) in
a #saucepan{}. Heat gently until steaming but not boiling, then remove from the
heat to produce =>hot cream{}.

== step make custard ==
Whisk ^egg yolks{5} with @granulated sugar{1/3%cup} until smooth and slightly
lightened. Slowly whisk in ^hot cream{}, starting with a small amount so the
yolks warm gradually.

Produce the =>custard{} by straining the mixture through a fine-mesh #strainer{} 
into a vessel with a spout, skim away any foam.

== step fill water bath ==
Divide ^custard{} evenly among the ramekins. Pour hot water into the roasting pan
until it reaches halfway up the sides of the ramekins. Take care not to splash
water into the custard.

== step bake ==
Bake at $temp{325%F} until the edges are set but the centers still jiggle gently,
about ~bake{30-40%minutes}, to produce =>baked custards{}.

== step chill ==
Carefully remove ^baked custards{} from the water bath and cool them at room
temperature for ~cool{30%minutes}. Cover and refrigerate for at least
~chill{4%hours}, or for up to 3 days.

== step brûlée ==
Blot any moisture from the chilled custards. Sprinkle each with a thin, even
layer of @granulated sugar{}(for the brûléed topping). Caramelize the sugar with
a #kitchen torch{} until deeply amber, bubbling, and crisp. Serve as soon as the
caramel hardens.
