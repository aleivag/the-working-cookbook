---
title: Indoor Clambake
yield: 4-6 servings
scale: yield
headnote: A stockpot clambake with lobster, shellfish, kielbasa, potatoes, and corn, layered so everything finishes cooking together.
tags: [seafood, clambake, lobster, shellfish, one-pot]
---

Choose a large, narrow stockpot so the ingredients can be layered easily. For a
half batch, use an 8-quart Dutch oven and keep the cooking time the same. Small
littleneck clams work best; if using larger clams, increase them to 4 pounds.

The cheesecloth is optional, but it makes the clams and mussels easier to remove.
The shellfish will release enough liquid to steam the other ingredients, so no
additional water is needed.

== step layer ==
Pack the ingredients as compactly as possible in a heavy-bottomed
#12-quart stockpot{}, adding them in the following layers:

- Arrange @kielbasa sausage{1%lb}(cut into 1/3-inch-thick rounds) with the cut
  sides against the bottom of the pot to create an insulating base.
- If using #cheesecloth{}, bundle @small littleneck clams{2%lb}(scrubbed) and
  @mussels{2%lb}(scrubbed and debearded), tying the ends securely. Pack the
  bundle—or the loose shellfish—into the smallest possible space.
- Add @small new potatoes{1%lb}(unpeeled and cut into 1-inch pieces).
- Add @corn{4%ears}(silk and all but the last layer of husk removed).
- Place @live lobsters{2}[weight_each=1.5 lb, scale=true] on top. They should be
  as fresh as possible.

== step steam ==
Cover the pot and place it over high heat. Cook for ~steam{17-20%minutes}, until
the potatoes are tender when tested with a #paring knife{} and the lobsters are
bright red.

== step arrange ==
Remove the pot from the heat and uncover it carefully, watching for escaping
steam. Remove the lobsters and set them aside until cool enough to handle.

Remove the corn, peel off the husks, and arrange it on a large platter. Using a
#slotted spoon{}, transfer the potatoes to the platter.

Transfer the shellfish bundle—or the loose shellfish, using the slotted spoon—to
a large bowl. If bundled, cut open the cheesecloth with #kitchen scissors{}.
Transfer the kielbasa to the platter, then pour the steaming liquid from the pot
over the clams and mussels.

== step serve ==
Holding the lobsters with a #dish towel{}, twist off the tails, claws, and legs.
Arrange the lobster pieces on the platter and serve immediately with
@salted butter{8%tbsp}(melted).
