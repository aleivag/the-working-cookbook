---
title: Croque Monsieur
yield: 4 servings
scale: yield
headnote: A classic French ham sandwich with crisp buttered bread, Gruyère, Parmesan, and a rich Mornay sauce.

Use a good-quality Gruyère for the best flavor and texture.

== step prepare oven ==
Adjust an #oven rack{} to 6 inches below the broiler element and heat the oven to
$temp{375%F}. Line a #rimmed baking sheet{} with #aluminum foil{} and coat it with
@vegetable oil spray{}.

== step toast bread ==
Brush both sides of @hearty white sandwich bread{8%slices} with
@unsalted butter{4%tbsp}(melted), then arrange the bread on the prepared sheet.

Bake until lightly golden on top, about ~first toast{10%minutes}. Flip the slices
and bake until the second side is golden, about ~second toast{3%minutes}. Reserve
4 slices for the sandwich tops and evenly space the remaining slices on the
sheet.

== step prepare Mornay sauce ==
Prepare @recipe{mornay-sauce}{1 1/4%cups} to produce
=>Mornay sauce{1 1/4%cups}. Keep it warm.

== step assemble ==
Spread ^Mornay sauce{4%tbsp}(1 tablespoon per slice) on the toast on the sheet. Fold
@thinly sliced Black Forest deli ham{12%oz} over itself several times to create
volume, then divide it evenly among the 4 slices.

Spread ^Mornay sauce{8%tbsp}(2 tablespoons per slice) on one side of each
reserved toast. Place them Mornay-side down over the ham.

== step top ==
Spread ^Mornay sauce{8%tbsp}(2 tablespoons per sandwich) over the sandwiches,
completely covering the toast and its edges so they do not burn under the
broiler. Sprinkle with
@Parmesan cheese{1/4%cup}(grated), followed by
@Gruyère cheese{4%oz}(shredded; about 1 cup).

== step bake and broil ==
Bake until the cheese is melted, about ~bake{5%minutes}. Turn on the broiler and
broil until the cheese bubbles across the tops and the edges are spotty brown,
about ~broil{5%minutes}. Serve immediately.
