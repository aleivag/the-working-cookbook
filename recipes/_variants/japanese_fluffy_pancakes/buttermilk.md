---
title: Buttermilk
yield: 4 pancakes
scale: yield
headnote: Tall, cloudlike pancakes made by folding a glossy meringue into a tender buttermilk batter.
source: Inspired by Japanese Fluffy Pancakes
source_url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7ZHRTnQxpzs
---

Use one large bowl for the primary batter. Fold the meringue gently so the egg
whites retain as much air as possible.

!youtube{7ZHRTnQxpzs}[title=Japanese-style fluffy pancake technique]

== step separate eggs ==
Separate @large eggs{3} to produce =>egg whites{3}, =>egg yolk{1}, and
=>extra egg yolks{2}. Refrigerate the extra yolks for another use.

== step make batter ==
In a large #mixing bowl{}, whisk @buttermilk{150%g}, ^egg yolk{1},
@butter{25%g}(melted), and @vanilla extract{1%tsp} until smooth.

Add @all-purpose flour{125%g}, @baking soda{1/4%tsp}, and
@baking powder{1/4%tsp}. Whisk just until no dry flour remains to produce
=>base batter{}.

== step make meringue ==
Using an #electric mixer{}, beat ^egg whites{3} with
@cream of tartar{1/4%tsp} until foamy. Gradually add @sugar{30%g} and continue
beating until the meringue is glossy and holds stiff peaks, producing
=>meringue{}.

== step fold ==
Whisk about one-third of ^meringue{} into ^base batter{} to lighten it. Using a
#silicone spatula{}, gently fold in the remaining meringue in two additions until
no large white streaks remain. Do not overmix. This produces =>pancake batter{}.

== step cook ==
Heat a lightly greased #nonstick skillet{} over low heat. For evenly shaped
pancakes, place greased #ring molds{} in the skillet.

Spoon ^pancake batter{} into 4 tall mounds or molds. Cover and cook gently until
the bottoms are golden and the sides begin to set, about ~first side{5-7%minutes}.
Carefully flip the pancakes, cover again, and cook until set through, about
~second side{4-5%minutes}.

== step serve ==
Serve immediately with @butter{} and @maple syrup{}, fruit, or another topping
of your choice.
