The sous vide bath cooks the fish gently and evenly. Drying it thoroughly before
searing is essential: the pan should brown the presentation side quickly without
overcooking the center.


== step cook the sea bass sous vide ==
Preheat a #sous vide water bath{} to $temp{132-134%F}.

Place @Chilean sea bass fillet{1} in a #vacuum bag{} and seal it. Cook in the
water bath for ~cook{30-45%minutes} to produce =>sous vide sea bass fillet{1}.

== step prepare the artichoke beurre monté ==
Prepare @recipe{artichoke-beurre-monte}{1%batch}[scale=false] and keep it warm to
produce =>artichoke beurre monté{1%batch}[scale=false].

== step sear the sea bass ==
Remove ^sous vide sea bass fillet{1} from the bag and pat it thoroughly dry.
Heat a #heavy skillet{} over high heat until very hot, then sear the presentation
side of the fillet until browned.

== step plate and finish ==
Spoon ^artichoke beurre monté{2%tbsp} onto a warm plate. Set the sea bass on top,
browned-side up. Drizzle with @extra-virgin olive oil{} and finish with
@fresh herbs{}(such as basil). Serve immediately.
