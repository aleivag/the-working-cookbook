title: Salmon Ceviche with Artichoke Leche de Tigre
yield: 1 batch
headnote: A composed salmon ceviche finished at serving time with a reusable artichoke leche de tigre.
tags: [ceviche, salmon, peruvian, seafood, draft]
---

The leche de tigre can be made ahead as its own recipe. Cut and season the salmon
separately, then combine everything immediately before serving.

== step leche de tigre ==
Prepare @recipe{artichoke-leche-de-tigre}{} and keep it refrigerated until service.

== step salmon ==
Cut @salmon{} into even bite-size cubes. Shortly before serving, season lightly
with @salt{} and keep cold.

== step assemble ==
At serving time, combine the salmon with enough chilled artichoke leche de tigre
to coat it generously. Taste, adjust the salt, and serve immediately.
