!youtube{LysF3BGtXt4}[title=Sous vide prime rib technique]

== step trim ==
Choose a @first-cut standing rib roast{7%lb}[3 bones] with an untrimmed fat cap,
ideally about 1/2 inch thick. Trim the fat cap to an even 1/2-inch layer if
necessary.

== step bones ==
Using a thin, sharp #knife{}, remove the rib bones by cutting down their length
and following their contours as closely as possible. Set the bones aside.

== step score ==
Cut slits in the fat cap at 1-inch intervals to form a crosshatch pattern. Cut
through the fat, but not into the meat.

== step salt ==
Rub @kosher salt{2%tbsp} over the entire roast and into the slits in the fat cap.

== step dry brine ==
Place the meat back on the bones to save refrigerator space. Set it on a plate
and refrigerate, uncovered, for ~dry brine{24-96%hours}.

== step preheat water bath ==
Preheat a #sous vide water bath{} to $temp{133%F}.

== step sear ==
Separate the meat from the bones again. Heat @vegetable oil{1%tbsp} in a
12-inch #skillet{} over medium-high heat until just smoking.

Sear the sides and fat cap of the roast until browned, about ~sear{6-8%minutes}.
Do not sear the side that was attached to the bones.

== step tie ==
Return the meat to the ribs, aligning the bones with the places from which they
were cut. Let the roast cool for ~cool{10%minutes}, then secure the meat to the
bones between the ribs with two lengths of #kitchen twine{}.

Season the roast with @black pepper{}.

== step sous vide ==
Place the roast in a 2-gallon #zipper-lock freezer bag{}. Lower the bag gently
into the water bath, allowing the water pressure to push out as much air as
possible. Clip the top corner to the side of the container, release any remaining
air, and seal the bag.

Cover the water bath and cook at $temp{133%F} for ~cook{16-24%hours}.

== step prepare broiler ==
Adjust an oven rack to the middle position and heat the broiler. Set a
#wire rack{} inside an aluminum foil-lined #rimmed baking sheet{} and coat the
rack with @vegetable oil spray{}.

== step rest and dry ==
Transfer the roast to the prepared rack, fat-side up, and let it rest for
~rest{10-15%minutes}. Pat the roast thoroughly dry with #paper towels{}.

Crumple a 12-inch piece of #aluminum foil{} into a 3-inch ball and place it under
the ribs to elevate the fat cap.

== step broil ==
Broil until the surface is deeply browned and crisp, about ~broil{4-8%minutes}.

== step carve ==
Transfer the roast to a #carving board{} and remove the twine. Cut the meat away
from the ribs and slice it 3/4 inch thick.
