Bistro Jeanty is a beautiful bistro in Yountville—my go-to whenever I am in
town. For years, I never tried their tomato soup because they have onion soup
on the menu, and I always ordered that instead. Today, however, I ordered the
tomato soup, and—oh my God—it was good. It turns out they sell the recipe on a
lovely postcard, which is the version shared here.

Use deeply ripe, juicy tomatoes. If the tomatoes are not especially ripe or
juicy, add the optional water while simmering the soup.

== step cook the tomatoes ==
Melt @unsalted butter{1/2%cup} in a large #stockpot{} over medium-low heat. Add
@yellow onions{1/2%lb}(sliced), cover, and cook for about ~soften onions{5%minutes}
without letting them color.

Add @ripe tomatoes{2 1/2%lb}(cored and quartered), @garlic{6%cloves},
@tomato paste{1/4%cup}, @bay leaf{1}, @whole black peppercorns{1/2%tbsp}, and
@dried thyme{1%tsp}. If the tomatoes are not ripe and juicy, add
@water{1%cup}(optional).

Simmer over low heat for ~simmer{30-40%minutes}, until the tomatoes and onions
are very soft, to produce =>cooked tomato mixture{}.

== step purée and finish the soup ==
Working in batches, purée ^cooked tomato mixture{} in a #blender{}, or purée it
in the pot with an #immersion blender{}. Pass it through a #fine-mesh strainer{}
and return it to the stockpot.

Stir in @heavy cream{4%cups}, @salt{}(to taste), @ground white pepper{1/2%tsp},
and @unsalted butter{1-2%tbsp}(to taste). Bring the soup back to a boil to
produce =>finished tomato soup{}.

== step chill the soup ==
Let ^finished tomato soup{} cool for ~cool{2%hours}, or refrigerate it overnight,
to produce =>chilled tomato soup{}.

== step make the egg wash ==
Beat @egg{1} with @water{1%tbsp} to produce =>egg wash{}.

== step prepare the pastry lids ==
Divide ^chilled tomato soup{} among six 8-ounce #ovenproof soup bowls{}.

Roll @puff pastry{1%lb}(thawed if frozen) to $thickness{1/4%inch}. Cut out 6
rounds slightly larger than the bowls.

Lightly brush one side of each pastry round with ^egg wash{}, then place it
egg-wash side down over a bowl. Pull the sides gently so the pastry is taut like
a drum.

== step bake and serve ==
Preheat the #oven{} to $temp{450%F}. Lightly brush the tops of the pastry rounds
with the remaining ^egg wash{} without pressing down on the dough.

Bake for ~bake{10-15%minutes}, until the pastry is puffed and golden brown. Do
not open the oven during the first few minutes, or the pastry may fall. Serve
immediately.
