Marraqueta is also known as *pan batido* or *pan francés* in different parts of
Chile. Its dough contains no fat; a very hot, steamy oven produces the thin,
crisp crust and light interior. Each marraqueta is made from two joined pieces
of dough and divided deeply across the center to form four connected lobes.

== step prepare the yeast [choice=yeast, option=instant, default=true] ==
Stir @instant yeast{5%g} and @granulated sugar{2.5%g} into
@water{300%g}(room temperature) to produce =>yeast water{300%g}.

== step prepare the yeast [choice=yeast, option=active-dry] ==
Warm about 50 g of @water{300%g} to approximately $temp{100%F}. Stir in
@active dry yeast{6%g} and @granulated sugar{2.5%g}, then let it stand for
~bloom{5-10%minutes}. Add the remaining water to produce
=>yeast water{300%g}.

== step prepare the yeast [choice=yeast, option=fresh] ==
Crumble @fresh yeast{15%g} into @water{300%g}(room temperature). Add
@granulated sugar{2.5%g} and stir until the yeast dissolves to produce
=>yeast water{300%g}.

== step mix and knead ==
Combine @bread flour{500%g} and @fine sea salt{10%g} in a large #mixing bowl{}.
Add ^yeast water{300%g} and mix until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest for
~rest{10%minutes}.

Knead the dough for ~knead{8-10%minutes}, until smooth, elastic, and only
slightly tacky. Do not add extra flour unless the dough is impossible to handle.
This produces =>marraqueta dough{}.

== step first rise ==
Place ^marraqueta dough{} in a clean #mixing bowl{}, cover it, and let it rise at
room temperature for ~first rise{60-90%minutes}, until approximately doubled in
volume, to produce =>risen marraqueta dough{}.

== step divide and preshape ==
Divide ^risen marraqueta dough{} into 16 equal pieces, approximately
$weight{50%g} each. Shape each piece into a tight ball, cover, and let the balls
rest for ~bench rest{15-20%minutes} to produce =>portioned marraqueta dough{16%pieces}.

== step shape the marraquetas ==
Line a #sheet pan{} with #parchment paper{}. Pair the balls from
^portioned marraqueta dough{16%pieces}, placing two together so they just touch,
then roll each pair lightly to make it slightly elongated.

Lightly coat a 3/4- to 1-inch #wooden dowel{} or thick wooden-spoon handle with
@neutral oil{}. Press it firmly across the center of each pair, almost to the
sheet pan, creating the deep groove that divides the bread into four connected
lobes. Remove the dowel carefully.

Cover the shaped rolls and proof for ~final proof{30-45%minutes}, until visibly
puffy. The central groove should remain distinct. This produces
=>proofed marraquetas{8}.

== step bake with steam ==
Place an empty #metal roasting pan{} on the lowest oven rack and preheat the
#oven{} to $temp{425%F}. Position another rack in the center.

Place ^proofed marraquetas{8} on the center rack. Carefully pour boiling
@water{} into the hot roasting pan and immediately close the oven to trap the
steam.

Bake for ~bake{16-20%minutes}, until deeply golden and crisp. Vent the oven
during the final few minutes for a drier crust. Transfer the marraquetas to a
#wire rack{} and cool briefly before serving.
