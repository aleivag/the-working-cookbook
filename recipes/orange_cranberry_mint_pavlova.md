This pavlova leaves several egg yolks behind. Use them in
[sous vide crème brûlée](recipe:sous-vide-creme-brulee), or browse
[other recipes that use egg yolks](search:egg-yolks).

== step heat whites ==
Combine @egg whites{3/4%cup}(from 5 to 6 eggs)[scale=true, step=0.25] and
@granulated sugar{1 1/2%cups} in the bowl of a #bain-marie{}. Whisk over the
simmering water until the mixture reaches at least $temp{140%F}, aiming for
somewhere close to $temp{160%F}. The sugar should be dissolved and the mixture
should feel smooth when rubbed between two fingers.

== step whip ==
Transfer the bowl to a #stand mixer{} fitted with the whisk attachment. Whip on
high speed for ~meringue{4%minutes}.

Add @vanilla extract{1%tsp}, @cornstarch{1 1/2%tsp}, and
@white vinegar{1 1/2%tsp}. Continue mixing just until evenly incorporated.

== step shape ==
Heat the oven to $temp{250%F}. Shape the meringue into a round on a
#parchment-lined baking sheet{}, building the edges slightly higher than the
center to hold the topping.

== step bake ==
Bake at $temp{250%F} for ~bake{1.5%hours}. Let the pavlova cool before moving or
topping it; the shell is fragile while warm.

== step finish ==
Top the cooled pavlova with @orange{}, @cranberries{}, and @fresh mint{} to taste.
Serve soon after topping so the shell remains crisp.
