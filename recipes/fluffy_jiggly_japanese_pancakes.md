This version uses very little flour and relies primarily on a stiff meringue for
its height. Serve the pancakes immediately because they gradually deflate as
they cool.

== step preheat pan ==
Preheat a #nonstick skillet{} over the lowest possible heat while preparing the
batter.

== step separate eggs ==
Separate @large eggs{2} to produce =>egg yolks{2} and =>egg whites{2}. Make sure
no yolk gets into the whites.

== step make yolk batter ==
In a large #mixing bowl{}, whisk ^egg yolks{2} with @milk{30%ml} and
@vanilla extract{1/2%tsp}(optional).

Sift in @all-purpose flour{24%g} and @baking powder{1%g}, then whisk just until
combined to produce =>yolk batter{}.

== step make meringue ==
In a clean bowl, beat ^egg whites{2} with @lemon juice{1/2%tsp}(optional) using
an #electric mixer{}. Add @sugar{26%g} in three additions and continue beating
until the meringue holds stiff peaks, producing =>meringue{}.

== step fold ==
Using a #silicone spatula{}, gently fold ^meringue{} into ^yolk batter{} without
deflating the egg whites to produce =>pancake batter{}.

== step cook ==
Brush the preheated skillet lightly with @cooking oil{}, then wipe away the
excess with a #paper towel{}. Pipe ^pancake batter{} into tall mounds using a
#piping bag{}.

Cover and cook over the lowest heat for about ~first side{4%minutes}. Check that
the bottoms are golden, then turn the pancakes carefully. Cover and cook the
second side for about ~second side{3%minutes}.

== step serve ==
Transfer the pancakes to a plate and serve immediately with @whipped cream{},
@jam{}, fruit, or another topping of your choice.
