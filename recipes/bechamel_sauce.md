---
title: Béchamel Sauce
yield: 1 cup
scale: yield
headnote: A smooth white sauce of butter, flour, and milk that serves as the base for Mornay and other sauces.
family: mother-sauce
tags: [sauce, french, mother-sauce, reusable]
---

== step make roux ==
Melt @unsalted butter{2%tbsp} in a #small saucepan{} over medium heat. Whisk in
@all-purpose flour{2%tbsp} and cook for ~roux{1%minute}.

== step add milk ==
Slowly whisk in @whole milk{1%cup}. Bring the sauce to a boil, whisking
constantly, then remove it from the heat. Use immediately or cover the surface
to prevent a skin from forming.
