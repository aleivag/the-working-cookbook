---
title: Simple Mapo Tofu
yield: 4 servings
scale: yield
tags: [tofu, sichuan, chinese, weeknight, vegetarian-option]
---

== step prepare ==
Separate the white and green parts of @scallions{12}. Smash the white parts and
slice them lengthwise. Thinly slice the green parts and reserve them for serving.

Cut @soft tofu{1%block}(or silken tofu) into bite-size squares. Put the tofu,
scallion whites, and @chicken broth{2%cups}(or another broth or water) in a
#microwave-safe bowl{}. Microwave for ~firm tofu{5-7%minutes}. Carefully drain
and reserve the broth. This step firms the delicate tofu before it goes into the
wok.

== step spice paste ==
Toast @Sichuan peppercorns{1%tsp}(plus more for serving) in the microwave until
fragrant, then grind them. Reserve a pinch for finishing the dish.

In a #food processor{}, grind @garlic{9%cloves}, @doubanjiang{1/3%cup}
(broad-bean chile paste; 豆瓣醬), @fresh ginger{3%inches}(sliced into coins), and
@fermented black beans{1%tbsp} into a coarse paste.

Add @vegetable oil{1/4%cup}, @Sichuan chile powder{1%tbsp}(or Korean chile powder),
and most of the ground Sichuan peppercorns. Process until combined.

== step meat ==
For the meat version, brown @ground beef or pork{}(optional; 85% lean or fattier)
in a hot #wok{}, breaking it into small pieces. Skip the meat for the vegetarian
version.

== step sauce ==
Add @vegetable oil{1%tbsp} to the wok, followed by the spice paste. Fry, stirring,
until the paste darkens slightly and the oil begins to separate.

Gently add the tofu and reserved broth. Stir in @hoisin sauce{2%tbsp} and
@toasted sesame oil{3%tbsp}. Fold carefully so the tofu does not break apart.

== step thicken ==
Bring the sauce to a boil. Mix @cornstarch{} with @water{} to make a smooth slurry,
then add it gradually until the sauce lightly coats the tofu. Simmer briefly after
each addition before deciding whether it needs more.

== step serve ==
Finish with the reserved scallion greens and ground Sichuan peppercorns. Serve
immediately with @steamed rice{}.
