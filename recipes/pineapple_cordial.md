== step mix cordial ==
Combine @clarified pineapple juice{830%g}, @citric acid{37%g}, and
@granulated sugar{230%g} in a #container{}. Stir or whisk until the citric acid
and sugar dissolve completely to produce =>pineapple cordial{1%batch}.

Keep refrigerated until needed.
