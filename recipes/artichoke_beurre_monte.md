---
title: Artichoke Beurre Monté
yield: 1 batch
headnote: A warm, buttery artichoke sauce brightened with lime and white pepper.
tags: [artichoke, sauce, butter, french]
---

This adds artichoke flavor to a classic beurre monté. I usually use jarred
artichoke hearts from Costco, but freshly cooked hearts work too. The sauce is
blended at the end, which makes the emulsion more forgiving.

This version uses roughly 2 parts butter to 1 part liquid. Chicken stock, wine,
or another flavorful liquid can replace the water; the artichoke and butter
should remain the dominant flavors.

== step prepare the beurre monté ==
Bring @chicken stock or water{1%tbsp} to a boil, then reduce the heat to low.
Gradually add @butter{2%tbsp}, a little at a time, while whisking constantly with
a #wire whisk{} to maintain the emulsion and produce =>beurre monté{}.

== step blend ==
Blend ^beurre monté{} with @cooked artichoke hearts{2} using an
#immersion blender{}. Add the juice of @lime{1/2}, @salt{}, and @white pepper{},
then blend until smooth to produce =>artichoke sauce{}.

== step strain ==
Strain ^artichoke sauce{} through a fine-mesh #strainer{} to remove any pieces of
artichoke that did not blend completely. Serve warm.
