Cooking the garlic three ways creates a layered flavor: most of the minced
garlic is slowly browned for sweetness, a smaller raw addition supplies bite,
and rehydrated garlic powder contributes roasted depth. Clarifying the butter
keeps its milk solids from browning while the garlic cooks.

Fresh Chinese egg noodles can replace the lo mein noodles. Alternatively, use
8 ounces of spaghetti. Liquid soy aminos can replace the Maggi seasoning, but
do not substitute soy sauce or coconut aminos. Use garlic powder rather than
granulated garlic.

== step clarify the butter ==
Cut @unsalted butter{8%tbsp} into 4 pieces and place it in a covered,
1-cup #microwave-safe liquid measuring cup{}. Microwave at 50 percent power for
~melt{1-2%minutes}, until melted.

Let the butter stand for ~separate{3-5%minutes}, until the clear yellow
butterfat separates from the whey. Spoon off 3 tablespoons of butterfat to
produce =>garlic-cooking butterfat{3%tbsp}. Reserve the remaining whey and
butterfat to produce =>finishing butter{5%tbsp}.

== step prepare the garlic paste and Parmesan ==
Combine @garlic powder{2%tsp}(not granulated garlic) with half of
@water{1%tbsp} in a small #bowl{} and stir until a smooth paste forms. Stir in
the remaining water to produce =>garlic powder paste{}.

Shred @Parmesan cheese{1 1/2%oz}(about 1/2 cup) on the large holes of a
#box grater{}, then crumble it into approximately 1/4-inch pieces. Measure
2 tablespoons to produce =>Parmesan for noodles{2%tbsp}, and reserve the rest
to produce =>Parmesan for serving{}.

== step cook the garlic ==
Transfer ^garlic-cooking butterfat{3%tbsp} to a 12-inch #skillet{}; a small
amount of whey is fine. Reserve 1 teaspoon from
@minced garlic{5%tsp}(divided), then add the remaining 4 teaspoons and
@kosher salt{1/2%tsp} to the skillet.

Cook over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, for ~brown garlic{7-10%minutes},
until the garlic is pale golden brown. Stir in the reserved teaspoon of minced
garlic, then immediately remove the skillet from the heat to produce
=>garlic butter{}.

== step cook the noodles ==
Bring @water{4%quarts}(for cooking the noodles) to a boil in a large #pot{}.
Add @fresh lo mein noodles{1%lb} and reduce the heat to maintain a very gentle
simmer.

Cook for ~cook noodles{5-7%minutes}, stirring occasionally, until almost tender;
the center of a noodle should remain firm with a slightly opaque dot. Drain well
to produce =>cooked noodles{}.

== step finish and serve ==
Add ^cooked noodles{} and @Maggi liquid seasoning{1%tbsp}(or liquid soy aminos)
to ^garlic butter{} in the skillet. Cook over medium-high heat, tossing with
#tongs{}, for about ~absorb seasoning{30%seconds}, until the seasoning is
absorbed and any large garlic clumps break apart.

Add @scallions{3}(thinly sliced), @sugar{1/4%tsp}, the remaining 5 tablespoons
of ^finishing butter{5%tbsp}, and ^garlic powder paste{}. Toss until thoroughly
combined, about ~finish sauce{30%seconds}.

Remove the skillet from the heat and season with @black pepper{}(to taste). Add
^Parmesan for noodles{2%tbsp} and toss to combine. Serve immediately, passing
^Parmesan for serving{} separately.
