== step prepare ==
Cut the tip from each @duck leg{} to expose the bone.

== step cure ==
Process @large onions{3}, @fresh thyme{12%sprigs}, @salt{2 1/2%tbsp},
@sugar{4 1/2%tsp}, and @black pepper{1 1/2%tsp} in a #food processor{}
until combined. Dried herbs, adobo powder, or another flavorful seasoning blend
can replace the fresh thyme.

Spread some of the onion mixture over the bottom of a #nonreactive pan{}. Arrange
the duck legs in a single layer with space between them, then cover them completely
with the remaining mixture. Cover and refrigerate for ~cure{4-6%days}.

== step sous vide ==
Carefully clean the cure from the legs without tearing the skin. Vacuum-seal the
legs in a #vacuum bag{}, keeping them in a single layer.

Cook in a #sous vide water bath{} at $temp{69%C} for ~confit{36%hours}.

== step separate ==
Remove the legs from the water bath. Open the bag and carefully transfer the legs
to a tray. Strain the bag liquid and separate the rendered @duck fat{} from the
concentrated juices. Reserve both; the juices make a rich duck tare.

== step crisp ==
Pat the duck legs completely dry without damaging the skin. Place them skin-side
up under a #broiler{} until the skin is deeply browned and crisp. Watch closely so
the skin does not burn.

== step serve ==
Plate and serve immediately. For a bright, savory plate, serve with bánh mì-style
pickles, herbs, and chiles. For a sweeter plate, pair with the accompaniments from
the citrus-mustard turkey confit preparation.
