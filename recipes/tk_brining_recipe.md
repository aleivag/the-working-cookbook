== step make brine ==
Combine @water{1%gallon}, @kosher salt{1%cup}, @honey{1/4%cup + 2%tbsp},
@bay leaves{12}, @garlic cloves{1/2%head}(skin on, smashed),
@black peppercorns{2%tbsp}, @rosemary sprigs{1/2%oz}(about 3 large),
@thyme{1/2%oz}(about 1 large bunch), @parsley{2%oz}(about 1 large bunch), and
@lemons{2}(zested and juiced) to produce =>brine{1%batch}.
