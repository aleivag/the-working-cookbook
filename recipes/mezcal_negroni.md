The pineapple cordial is prepared as a full batch regardless of the number of
cocktails. Measure 25 ml from the finished batch for each drink.

== step prepare pineapple cordial ==
Prepare @recipe{pineapple-cordial}{1%batch}[scale=false] to produce
=>pineapple cordial{1%batch}[scale=false].

== step mix cocktail ==
Combine @mezcal{25%ml}, @Campari{20%ml},
@Cocchi Vermouth di Torino{25%ml}, and ^pineapple cordial{25%ml} in a
#mixing glass{}. Add @ice{} and stir until well chilled.

Strain into a #rocks glass{} over fresh ice and serve immediately.
