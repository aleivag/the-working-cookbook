---
title: Shrimp Cocktail
tags: []
headnote: Tender shrimp and creamy avocado in a bright, chilled lime-and-tomato broth.
---

== step prepare shrimp ==
Separate the heads from the @shrimp{6} to produce =>shrimp heads{6}. Peel and
devein the shrimp to produce =>shrimp tails{6}. Discard the shells.

== step make shrimp stock ==
In a pot over high heat, add @butter{1%tbsp} and @olive oil{1%tbsp}. Once the
butter has melted, add ^shrimp heads{6} and stir until they begin to brown.

Add @water{100%ml} and @salt{1%pinch}, then mash the heads with a press to extract
all their juices and produce =>shrimp stock{}.

== step cook the shrimp ==
Add ^shrimp tails{6} to ^shrimp stock{} and cook, stirring as needed, until the
shrimp cook evenly.

Remove the shrimp tails and chop them to produce =>chopped shrimp tails{}. Strain
the stock to produce =>strained shrimp stock{}, pressing the heads thoroughly
before discarding them.

== step make the cocktail ==
Combine the juice of @limes{3}, ^strained shrimp stock{100%ml},
@tomato juice{}(V8 or Clamato), @cilantro{}, @celery{}, @avocado{1}(diced), and
^chopped shrimp tails{}.

Refrigerate the cocktail for ~chill{15-30%minutes}.
