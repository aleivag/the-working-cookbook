== step prepare shrimp ==
Separate the heads from the @shrimp{6} and reserve them. Then peel and devein the
shrimp tails. Reserve the tails and discard the shells.

== step make head stock ==
In a pot over high heat, add @butter{1%tbsp} and @olive oil{1%tbsp}. Once the
butter has melted, add the shrimp heads and stir until they begin to brown.

Add @water{100%ml} and @salt{1%pinch}, then mash the heads with a press to extract
all their juices.

== step cook the shrimp ==
Add the tails to the shrimp head stock and let them cook, stirring as needed so
that they cook evenly.

Remove the shrimp tails from the stock and chop them. Then strain the stock,
keeping only the liquid and discarding the heads.

== step make the cocktail ==
Add the juice of @lime{3}, @V8{}, or @Clamato{}, along with @cilantro{},
@celery{}, @avocado{1} cut into cubes, and the chopped shrimp.

Put the cocktail in the refrigerator to chill for 15 to 30 minutes.
