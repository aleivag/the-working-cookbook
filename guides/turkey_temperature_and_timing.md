## Sous vide turkey breast

For sous vide cooking, thickness determines how long the center takes to heat
and pasteurize; total breast weight is not a reliable timing measurement. The
following targets come from
[Serious Eats' sous vide turkey breast guide](https://www.seriouseats.com/sous-vide-turkey-breast-crispy-skin-recipe-thanksgiving).

| Final result | Bath temperature | Core temperature | Time after reaching core | Approximate total time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Very pink, soft, extra moist | $temp{132%F} | $temp{130%F} | $time{2%hours} | $time{4%hours} |
| Pale pink, soft, moist | $temp{138%F} | $temp{136%F} | $time{1%hour} | $time{3%hours} |
| White, tender, moist | $temp{145%F} | $temp{143%F} | $time{16%minutes} | $time{2 1/2%hours} |
| White with a traditional roast texture | $temp{152%F} | $temp{150%F} | $time{4%minutes} | $time{2%hours} |

Use a validated pasteurization table to determine the required time from the
meat's thickness, starting temperature, and bath temperature.
